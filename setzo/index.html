A look inside Setzo

What you can do with Setzo

Log your training

Record exercises, sets, routines, workout notes, runs and walks, with rest timers and a Lock Screen Live Activity.

See your progress

Review workout history and personal records. Use local tracking without an account, or create an account for optional cloud sync.

Explore meal ideas

Keep a Nutrition Passport on your device and request Fuel Buddy dish suggestions. Meal requests and selected food preferences are processed as described in the Privacy Policy.
